The Auralis Method

Four steps, one principle:
Understand it scientifically. Apply it holistically.

The Auralis Method brings the precision of scientific work to change in everyday life. We don’t just look at what you want to change — but why something no longer fits, which connections become visible and which steps are genuinely doable for you.

Step I

Listen

A thorough assessment of your history, your current situation, your habits and your goals. In the Root Session we take time to truly understand your starting point.

Step II

Analyse

Your answers are analysed individually. We look at possible connections between nutrition, stress, sleep, energy, movement, rhythm and lifestyle — not in isolation, but as part of your everyday life.

Step III

Align

The analysis becomes a personal report with clear recommendations and first actionable steps. From it we develop a plan for nutrition, habits and lifestyle that fits your body and your life.

Step IV

Sustain

In The Bloom or The Flourishing we guide you through implementation. We work with concrete weekly goals, adjust your plan continuously and anchor new routines step by step in your everyday life.

The method combines nutritional science, holistic health guidance, behaviour change and everyday self-regulation — clear, individual and actionable. As needed, breathwork, meditation or gentle movement also flow in.

The founder

Understand the body. See the person.

Auralis Natura grew out of a very personal conviction: health may be well-founded, but it may also feel human. It needs science — and, at the same time, time, listening, trust and an eye for the life behind the symptoms, routines and questions.

I’m Desiree Gruber, a chemist with a doctorate. My master’s and doctorate were in organic and bioorganic chemistry. Twelve years in the pharmaceutical industry — in research, manufacturing and quality assurance — shaped how I work: precise, structured, evidence-oriented and with high standards of quality.

At the same time, I increasingly sensed that health doesn’t live only in data, values or single recommendations. It shows up in real life: in what we eat, how we sleep, how we deal with stress, the rhythm of our days and the life stage we’re in.

That’s why I expanded my scientific foundation with training in holistic nutrition and health, holistic women’s health, and yoga and meditation. Today I combine scientific clarity with a holistic view of the body, nutrition, habits and everyday life.

With Auralis Natura I want to create a space where you feel seen and taken seriously. A space where we don’t look for standard solutions, but for your path. Together we develop a plan that fits you, your body and your real life — clear, doable and personal.
