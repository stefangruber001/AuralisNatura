The Auralis Method

Four steps, one principle:
Understand it scientifically. Apply it holistically.

Behind every habit, every symptom and every challenge there are connections. The Auralis method helps make those connections visible, place them in a scientific context and translate them into concrete changes that work in everyday life.

Step I

Listen

Every change begins with understanding. In the Root Session we take time for your story, your current situation, your habits and your goals. Together we build the foundation for truly understanding your starting point.

Step II

Analyse

Your answers are analysed individually. We look at possible connections between nutrition, sleep, stress, energy, movement and lifestyle — not in isolation, but in the context of your everyday life and your personal situation.

Step III

Align

Insight becomes clarity. Building on it, we develop concrete recommendations and an individual path that fits your body, your needs and your everyday life.

Step IV

Anchor

In The Bloom and The Flourishing we help you anchor new habits for the long term. Together we adjust the path when challenges appear, and create changes that can hold in your everyday life.

Focus on women’s health

Women’s health for every life stage and every everyday life.

Women’s health deserves individual solutions rather than blanket advice.

Many women go through phases where energy, resilience and wellbeing can no longer be taken for granted. The female body changes over the course of life and is shaped by hormonal processes, professional demands, family, stress and personal transitions.

This isn’t about functioning the same way all the time. It’s about understanding your own body better and shaping nutrition, habits, recovery and rhythm so they suit the life stage you are in.

Especially for women with demanding schedules, in leadership roles, self-employed or under high mental load, health doesn’t come from perfection — it comes from solutions that fit and last.

The founder

Understand the body. See the person.

Auralis Natura grew out of a simple conviction: health needs a scientific foundation and, at the same time, time, listening and trust. Because behind every symptom, habit and question there is always a person with their own story.

I’m Desiree Gruber, a chemist with a doctorate. More than fifteen years in research and the pharmaceutical industry have shaped the way I work: precise, structured and evidence-based.

At the same time, I increasingly sensed that health doesn’t live only in data, values or single recommendations. It shows up in real life: in what we eat, how we sleep, how we deal with stress, the rhythm of our days and the life stage we’re in.

So I complemented my scientific foundation with training in nutrition, women’s health, yoga and meditation. Today I combine scientific clarity with a holistic view of health, habits and everyday life.

With Auralis Natura I want to create a space where people feel seen and taken seriously. Together we develop a path that fits your body, your everyday life and your life stage — clear, doable and personal.
