The Auralis Method

Four steps, one principle:
understand the science, apply it as a whole.

Every habit, every symptom and every difficulty has something behind it. The Auralis method brings those connections into view, sets them in scientific context and turns them into changes that work in everyday life.

Step I

Listen

Every change begins with understanding. In Clarity we take our time over your story, where you are now, the habits you’ve built and what you want to reach. That’s the ground we need to see your starting point clearly.

Step II

Analyse

Your answers are analysed individually. We look at how nutrition, sleep, stress, energy, movement and lifestyle may be connected — never in isolation, but in the context of your daily life and your own situation.

Step III

Align

Insight turns into clarity. From there we shape concrete recommendations and a path of your own — one that suits your body, your needs and your everyday life.

Step IV

Anchor

In Change and Balance we help you make new habits stick. When difficulties come up we adjust the route together, so the changes can hold their place in your life.

Focus: women’s health

Women’s health for every stage and every kind of day.

Women’s health deserves individual answers, not blanket advice.

Many women go through phases where energy, resilience and wellbeing can no longer be taken for granted. A woman’s body changes over a lifetime, shaped by hormones, work, family, stress and the transitions in between.

The goal isn’t to perform the same way every day. It’s to understand your own body better and to shape food, habits, rest and rhythm around the stage of life you’re actually in.

Especially for women with demanding days — leading teams, running their own business, or carrying a heavy mental load — health doesn’t come from being perfect. It comes from solutions that fit and can be kept up.

The founder

Understand the body. See the person.

Auralis Natura began with a simple conviction: good health needs solid science, and it needs time, listening and trust. Because behind every symptom, every habit and every question there is a person with a story of their own.

I’m Desiree Gruber, a chemist with a doctorate. More than fifteen years in research and the pharmaceutical industry shaped the way I work: precise, structured, evidence-based.

At the same time I came to feel, more and more strongly, that health doesn’t live only in data, numbers or isolated advice. It shows up in real life: in what we eat, how we sleep, how we handle stress, the rhythm our days take and the stage of life we happen to be in.

So I built on my scientific grounding with training in nutrition, women’s health, yoga and meditation. Today I bring scientific clarity together with a whole view of health, habits and daily life.

With Auralis Natura I want to create a space where people feel seen and taken seriously. Together we shape a path that suits your body, your daily life and the stage you’re in — clear, workable and entirely your own.
