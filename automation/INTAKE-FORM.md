# Secure Intake — Google Form question set

**Form settings:** Collect email · Limit to 1 response · Show progress bar · Confirmation message: “Thank you — Desiree will prepare your report. You'll hear back personally.” · Responses → `Auralis CRM`. Store in EU (Workspace data region = Europe).

> Compliance: this is holistic‑health **coaching & education, not medical care**. The red‑flag screen (Section C) must stay; if anything is ticked, the report opens by recommending a doctor; emergencies → 112.

## Section A — About you
1. Full name (short answer, required)
2. Age (short answer)
3. Location & preferred language — EN / DE / ES (multiple choice)
4. In your own words, what would you most like to change? (paragraph, required)
5. Why now? (paragraph)
6. What have you already tried? (paragraph)

## Section B — Your daily life
7. Energy level today (linear scale 1–5)
8. Sleep quality (1–5)
9. Stress level (1–5)
10. Digestion / gut comfort (1–5)
11. Anything to add on the above? (paragraph)
12. A typical day of eating (paragraph)
13. Movement / sport (short answer)
14. Life stage (e.g. perimenopause, postpartum, high‑performance, general) (short answer)
15. Supplements you take (short answer)

## Section C — Safety screen (required)
16. Diagnosed conditions (paragraph)
17. Medications (paragraph)
18. Allergies / intolerances (short answer)
19. Pregnant or breastfeeding? (Yes / No / N/A)
20. **Please tick anything that currently applies** (checkboxes, required — include “None of these”):
    - Unexplained weight loss
    - Chest pain or breathlessness
    - Severe or persistent pain
    - Fainting / blackouts
    - Thoughts of self‑harm
    - An unhealthy relationship with food
    - None of these
21. Anything a doctor is currently investigating? (paragraph)

## Section D — Consent (all required checkboxes)
22. ☐ I understand Auralis Natura provides holistic‑health coaching & education — **not** medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
23. ☐ I consent to Auralis Natura processing the health information I provide to prepare my personalised report, in line with the Privacy Policy (GDPR).
24. ☐ (optional) I'm happy to receive occasional follow‑ups about my plan.
