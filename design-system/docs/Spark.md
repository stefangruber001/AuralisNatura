# Spark

Three dots — clay, then a slightly larger amber one, then clay again. The
quietest signature in the brand, inherited from the printed documents where it
separates movements of an argument.

Use it to punctuate: between a hero and the credentials ribbon, above a closing
line, at the end of a long section. Do not put one on every block — repeated, it
stops being punctuation and becomes wallpaper.

```jsx
<Spark />
<Spark large />
```

`large` is the standalone section ornament, centred with air around it. The
default size sits inline with type.
