# Writing copy for this system

Voice: warm, intelligent, calm, precise — *a brilliant friend who happens to be
a scientist*. Never breathless, never a promise of transformation, never a
countdown timer.

## Language

**German is the master language.** English and Spanish are re-derived from the
German, not translated word by word — the German sentence sets the meaning and
the rhythm, and the other two are written to sound native rather than parallel.
Spanish is written gender-neutral where the German is.

Never break a word. `hyphens` is off across the system; German compounds are
handled by reducing type size, not by splitting words. Check every headline at
360px before shipping it.

## Sentence shapes that fit the components

- `Display` — two short sentences, the second in `Em`. "Verstehe deinen Körper.
  *Verbessere deine Gesundheit nachhaltig.*"
- `Text variant="lead"` — one sentence, stating what the section is for.
- `PackageCard description` — bold the first sentence: it is the promise, and it
  is the only line a scanner reads.
- `CredentialChip` — a noun phrase, no verb, one verifiable fact.
- `ImageBand caption` — one editorial sentence, never a paragraph.

## Claims

Every claim must survive being checked. This is a regulated space:

- Coaching and health education, never diagnosis, treatment, or medical
  nutrition therapy.
- "Dr." is `Dr. rer. nat.` — a doctorate in chemistry, disclosed as such, never
  implying a physician.
- Complement medical care; refer out; keep the emergency note.
- Testimonials are real or absent. There is no third option.

Prefer "kann unterstützen" to "beseitigt". Where evidence is mixed, say so — on
this brand, admitting uncertainty is the credibility.
