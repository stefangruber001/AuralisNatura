# Label

The small uppercase, wide-tracked line that opens a section or annotates a
value. It is the design system's orientation device: it tells the reader what
kind of thing is coming before they read a word of the heading.

Clay on light surfaces; warm sand (`--sage-soft`) on the dark bands — pass
`onDark` there, or the label disappears into the brown.

`kicker` adds the short gold rule that marks a section opening. Use it on the
label that starts a section, not on labels used inside cards.

```jsx
<Label kicker>Die Gründerin</Label>

<Label onDark>Wege der Zusammenarbeit</Label>
```

Keep labels to two or three words. They are read at a glance, and tracking that
wide turns a long label into a decoration nobody parses.
