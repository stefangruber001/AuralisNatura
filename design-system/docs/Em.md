# Em

The brand's second voice: Fraunces italic, cinnamon-rust on light surfaces and
amber gold on the dark bands.

`Em` marks **the turn in a headline** — the clause where the sentence changes
direction, from problem to promise. That is its whole job.

```jsx
<Heading level={2}>Den Körper verstehen. <Em>Den Menschen sehen.</Em></Heading>
```

Do not set a whole paragraph in `Em`; at paragraph length the italic reads as a
pull-quote the reader has to work through. For emphasis inside running text use
`<strong>` instead.
