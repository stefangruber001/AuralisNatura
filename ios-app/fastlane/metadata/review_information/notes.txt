Auralis Natura is the client companion for a one-to-one holistic-health coaching practice run by Dr. rer. nat. Desiree Gruber (Barcelona).

NO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED TO EXPLORE THE APP. On first launch it opens in guest mode: the programmes, the booking calendar and the published "Impulse" articles are all browsable without signing in. The demo credentials in the fields above open the client area (questionnaire, personal report, appointments) so you can review those screens too.

PAYMENTS: the programmes are real-world, human-delivered coaching — scheduled one-to-one video sessions plus a report Dr. Gruber writes and reviews personally. Under guideline 3.1.3(d) (person-to-person services) and 3.1.3(e) (services consumed outside the app) these are collected outside in-app purchase, via Stripe. The app sells no digital content, unlocks no in-app features by payment, and contains no IAP. The corporate/group offer is enquiry-only by email and has no purchase path.

HEALTH DATA: everything is self-reported by the client, encrypted at rest, and processed under GDPR with explicit consent. The app is coaching and education, not medical care, and says so; it does not diagnose or treat. Emergency guidance (112) is included in the client-facing material.

SERVER: the app talks to https://api.auralisnatura.com. If a screen shows a network error, please retry — the backend is self-hosted.
