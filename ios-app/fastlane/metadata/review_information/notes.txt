This app is the client companion for a 1:1 holistic-health coaching practice. Sign-in required — a demo login is provided in the review fields. Purchases are for real-world, human-delivered coaching services (sessions + a personally-reviewed report), so payment uses Stripe per guideline 3.1.3(d); the app does not sell digital content. Health/wellbeing data is self-reported, encrypted, GDPR-based.
