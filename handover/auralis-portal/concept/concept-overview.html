The idea in one line

A client logs in, fills a beautiful health intake; Desiree reviews it, adds her call notes, and clicks one button; a cloud agent drafts a super-premium, science-led report; she edits and approves it; a second click renders the PDF and drops a ready email — with a booking link for the review call — into her Gmail drafts to send.

The golden rule. The agent only ever produces a draft. Nothing reaches a client until Desiree has reviewed, edited and clicked Generate. This is coaching & education — never medical diagnosis — and the human-approval gate is wired into the process.

How the report gets made

Intake + call notes → Cloud Agent → your review → premium PDF

The console sends the agent only what it needs (minimised intake + your notes). The agent returns a structured six-part draft — Your starting point · What we're seeing · The science, simply · Your plan · When to see a doctor · Your next steps — benchmarked against the best holistic reports, with charts built from the client's own energy/sleep/stress numbers. You edit it, approve it, and the renderer turns it into a visually rich, on-brand PDF.

Hosting, domain & safety

Site → Cloudflare Pages (dev/prod previews; replaces GitHub Pages). Portal + console + agent → a local Flask app on Desiree's Mac (→ Windows later), reachable only through a Cloudflare named tunnel at api.auralisnatura.com — the app never opens a public port. The console sits behind Cloudflare Access (email code). Recommendation: move the domain from Squarespace (expensive) to Cloudflare Registrar (wholesale price + one control plane + the security tunnel).
