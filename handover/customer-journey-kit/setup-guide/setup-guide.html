1 · What you're setting up

A premium, mostly-automated practice where the software does the busywork and you do only the two things that need a human: the conversation and the judgement.

The journey has 11 steps. Only two are manual (the discovery call and the report walk-through) and one is your approval gate (you review and edit every report before it goes out). The other eight run themselves.

3 · Install the kit on your MacBook

  1. Copy the whole customer-journey-kit folder into your Documents (e.g. ~/Documents/Auralis/customer-journey-kit). Keep the sub-folders together — the templates load the brand seal from assets/.
  2. Install Google Chrome (the templates print to PDF most reliably from Chrome). google.com/chrome
  3. Open any template by double-clicking it — it opens in your browser. Nothing to install, no code to run.

4 · Step 1–2 · Booking & payment (Cal.com + Stripe)

  1. In Stripe: finish account activation (business details, your IBAN). Note: never share your secret key (sk_…) with anyone.
  2. In Cal.com: connect Stripe (Settings → Apps → Stripe → Connect).
  3. Create the event types:
    • Free discovery call — 25 min, no payment, Google Meet location.
    • The Root Session €198, The Bloom €398, The Flourishing (starts with a free intro call) — for paid events, turn on “Requires payment” so Stripe collects at booking (this cuts no-shows dramatically).
  4. Brand the widget with the Auralis colours: espresso #3D2719, clay #A8492A, cream #FBF6EB.
  5. Send me (or paste into the site) the event links so every “Book a free call” button on the website points to the live calendar.
On the website the booking buttons are already wired to a Google Calendar appointment link as a stand-in. Once your Cal.com links are ready, they replace that in one step.

5 · Step 6 · Intake form (Tally)

  1. In Tally, create a new form and add the questions from claude/intake-questions-EN.md (and a German version from intake-questions-DE.md — or one form with both).
  2. Keep the red-flag tick-list and the two required consent checkboxes exactly as written — they are your safety + GDPR layer. Link your Privacy Policy on the consent question.
  3. Tally → Integrations → Webhook: this is how a submission starts the automation (you'll paste the Make webhook URL here in the next step).
  4. Copy the public form URL — it goes in the welcome email.

6 · Step 5 + 11 · The automation glue (Make)

Make is the conductor. You build one “scenario” (a visual flow) once; then it runs forever.

The onboarding scenario

Trigger: Stripe payment succeeded (or Cal.com booking paid) →
then: send the welcome email (with the Tally intake link) · create a client row in your records · book the report session.

The report scenario

Trigger: Tally submission → send the intake to the Claude “Auralis Report Engine” → return the draft to you (email/Notion). It stops here — at your approval gate. It never sends anything to the client.

The money + loop scenario

Trigger: you mark “delivered” → Stripe invoice/receipt → (after a few days) the review-request email.

  1. In Make, create a scenario, add the Webhook module, copy its URL, and paste it into Tally (and/or Stripe/Cal.com) as above.
  2. Add the email module (connect Gmail), the Claude module (paste your Anthropic API key into Make's secure field — it stays in Make, not in any file), and a “records” module (Google Sheets or Notion).
  3. Minimise PII: where possible pass links and record IDs between tools, not raw health details.
Start simpler if you like. You can run the whole journey manually first (you paste intake into Claude yourself, send emails yourself) and add Make scenarios one at a time as each step becomes a bottleneck. The kit works either way.

7 · Step 7 · The AI report engine (Claude)

  1. In Claude (claude.ai) create a Project named “Auralis Report Engine”.
  2. Open claude/report-engine-system-prompt.md, copy the text between the lines, and paste it into the Project's Instructions.
  3. In Settings, turn off any “use my data for training” option.
  4. Per client: start a new chat in the Project and paste the intake with: “Here is the client's intake — please draft the report:”

8 · Step 8–9 · Your approval + the premium PDF

This is the heart of the practice and the one step that is always yours.

  1. Read Claude's draft. Edit anything — tone, accuracy, safety. If the intake shows a red flag, make sure the report opens by recommending a doctor.
  2. Open templates/report-EN.html (or report-DE.html) in Chrome.
  3. Click any dashed box and paste your approved text. Fill the client name, date, and the six sections.
  4. Press ⌘P → Destination “Save as PDF” → open More settings → turn ON “Background graphics” (critical, or the colours won't print) → Margins “Default” → Save.
  5. Reopen the fresh template for the next client (your edits live only in the tab).
The grey instructions box and dashed outlines never appear in the PDF — only the clean report does.

9 · Step 10 · Deliver & discuss

  1. Email the PDF personally — use template 3 in emails-EN/DE.md.
  2. Walk through it together on the booked video session. The report is the script; the session is the warmth.

10 · Step 11 · Invoice (Stripe) + the template

  1. Preferred: Stripe creates the invoice/receipt automatically on payment. For Spanish IVA-correct invoices, connect Quaderno or hand Stripe data to your gestor.
  2. Backup / manual: open templates/invoice-EN.html (or DE), click the dashed fields, fill the number, client, line item and totals, then ⌘P → Save as PDF (Background graphics ON).
Spain — confirm with your gestor: autónomo registration, the cuota, IRPF, and the IVA (VAT) treatment of coaching (it is not automatically exempt). Add your NIF and registered address to the invoice template's “From” block before sending any real invoice.

11 · Step 11 (loop) · The review request

A well-timed, no-pressure ask turns happy clients into the proof that fuels the next free call. Use template 5. Add genuine reviews to the website as they arrive — never invent reviews.

12 · Your daily rhythm

Per new client, ~20 focused minutes of admin:

  1. Take the discovery call (manual, 25 min).
  2. Client pays → automation sends welcome + intake automatically.
  3. Intake arrives → Claude draft lands in your inbox.
  4. You: review, edit, approve → paste into the report template → Save as PDF (~15 min).
  5. Email + walk-through session (manual).
  6. Invoice + review request fire automatically.

13 · Compliance guardrails — keep these sacred

  • Coaching & education, never medical care. No diagnosis, no treatment of disease, no prescriptive medical nutrition therapy.
  • “Dr.” = Dr. rer. nat. (academic doctorate in chemistry), not a physician — always stated transparently.
  • Refer out. Keep the red-flag screen and the “see your doctor / call 112” guidance in the intake and every report.
  • Human approval gate on every client output (step 8) — non-negotiable.
  • GDPR: EU hosting, explicit consent, data minimisation, defined retention; health data is special-category.
  • Real testimonials only.
