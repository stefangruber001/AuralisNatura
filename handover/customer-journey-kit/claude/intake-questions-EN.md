# Intake questionnaire — English (paste into Tally)

## A — About you
1. Full name *(short text, required)*
2. Age *(number)*
3. Location & preferred language — DE / EN / ES *(short text)*
4. In your own words, what is your main goal right now? *(long text, required)*
5. Why now — what made you reach out today? *(long text)*
6. What have you already tried? *(long text)*

## B — Your everyday life
7. Energy (1–5) + a note *(linear scale + short text)*
8. Sleep (1–5) + a note
9. Stress (1–5) + a note
10. Digestion (1–5) + a note
11. A typical day of eating *(long text)*
12. Movement / exercise *(short text)*
13. Life stage *(e.g. cycle, fertility, pregnancy, postpartum, perimenopause)*
14. Supplements you take *(long text)*

## C — Safety (the red-flag screen)
15. Any diagnosed conditions? *(long text)*
16. Current medications? *(long text)*
17. Allergies / intolerances? *(long text)*
18. Pregnant or breastfeeding? *(yes / no / not applicable)*
19. **Please tick anything that applies** *(checkboxes — required):*
    - Unexplained weight loss
    - Chest pain or breathlessness
    - Severe or persistent pain
    - Fainting / blackouts
    - Thoughts of self-harm
    - An unhealthy relationship with food
    - **None of the above**
20. Anything your doctor is currently investigating? *(long text)*

## D — Consent (required)
21. ☑ I understand Auralis Natura provides holistic health **coaching and education**, not
    medical care, and does not diagnose, treat or replace a doctor. *(required checkbox)*
22. ☑ I consent to Auralis Natura processing my personal and health data (special-category
    data under the EU GDPR) to deliver my coaching, stored securely and never shared without
    my consent. *(required checkbox — link your Privacy Policy)*

> In an emergency, contact your local emergency services — in Spain, **112**.
