Fig. 01 — Vision, Mission & Model

Clarity of purpose, clarity of mechanism

Mission

To help people reclaim their energy and health through coaching that is as rigorous as it is human.

Vision

To become the most trusted name in premium, science-led holistic health for women — a brand people are proud to recommend.

The business model in one line

A productised expert service: high-trust, premium 1:1 coaching, made scalable by a systemised, AI-assisted report and a recurring membership.

  • Acquire via free call + referrals + credible content.
  • Convert with authority, a clear ladder, and a stunning deliverable.
  • Retain through results, relationship, and the Companion membership.
  • Leverage with systems so revenue isn't strictly hours × rate.
Design principle Every choice optimises for trust per hour and revenue per hour — because the founder's time is the scarcest, most valuable resource in the business.
Fig. 02 — The Offer Ladder & Pricing

From free call to lasting relationship

A deliberate ascension: remove the risk of the first step, then offer increasing depth of support. Prices sit confidently in the premium band — credentials and the deliverable justify them, and under-pricing would undercut the brand.

Pricing logic

Anchored & premium

The €1,290 flagship anchors the range and makes The Reset feel accessible. Benchmarks for comparable 3-month packages commonly run €1,200–1,500, with monthly memberships €100–200 — Auralis sits squarely, credibly premium.

Instalments

Lower the barrier, keep the price

Offer 2–3 instalments on programmes (e.g. 3 × €450 for The Transformation). This widens access without discounting the premium positioning.

The deliverable as value

Why it commands the price

Each engagement produces a beautiful, reasoned personal report. It makes the intangible tangible, justifies premium pricing, and is the thing clients screenshot and share.

Pricing discipline Resist the instinct to discount early. A premium science-led brand that competes on price destroys its own positioning. Use instalments, a smaller entry offer, and the free call to manage affordability — never a lower sticker price. Raise prices as testimonials and demand accumulate.

Key insight 01

Programmes beat sessions. The Reset and Transformation roughly double the effective hourly rate of a single session — guide clients up the ladder, don't sell sessions in isolation.

Membership compounds. Each Companion client adds predictable monthly revenue at a high hourly rate — the path to income that isn't re-earned from zero each month.

Capacity reality-check The Base-case year below requires roughly ~230 client-hours across the year — about 4–5 hours per week of delivery (plus admin and marketing). That is deliberately achievable alongside full-time employment and a new baby.

Fig. 05 — Go-to-Market & Marketing

How clients find — and choose — Auralis

A premium practice does not need a loud megaphone; it needs the right people to arrive already half-convinced. The strategy is built for trust and word-of-mouth, not paid volume — and to run on a few protected hours a week.

The funnel — one engine, three jobs

Step 01 · Attract

Be findable & credible

A small amount of high-quality content, a sharp niche, and a few referral relationships bring the right visitors to the site — already primed by the founder's rare credentials.

Step 02 · Convert

The free 25′ call

Every path leads to one action: book a free first session. No price objection, no commitment — just a warm, expert conversation that earns trust and surfaces fit.

Step 03 · Ascend

Package → membership → referral

The call converts to a package; the package's results convert to a membership and a five-star review; happy clients convert their friends. The loop compounds.

The single most important number

Everything is engineered to maximise one metric: free calls booked — then the percentage of those calls that become paying clients. Optimise the call, and the whole business grows.

The referral engine — the real growth flywheel

The single most valuable asset is a network of 5–10 referral partners who serve the same woman from a different angle and have no scientific-nutrition offer of their own. Each is a relationship, not a transaction: offer genuine value (a talk for their clients, a clear scope of who to send), make referring effortless, and close the loop with a thank-you. One good partner can outperform months of content.

Multilingual reach as an unfair advantage

German, English, Spanish and Italian open four markets from one practice — and a uniquely under-served one: English- and German-speaking expats in Barcelona and remote clients across Europe who want science-literate guidance in their own language. The site speaks all three core languages; content can be seeded in whichever market responds first.

Content principle — authority, not volume Publish less, but unmistakably expert. A handful of genuinely rigorous pieces — "what the evidence actually says about X" — does more for a premium brand than daily posting. Every piece should sound like it could only come from someone who has read the primary literature, because it does.

Fig. 07 — Roadmap & Milestones

A staged, part-time launch

Sequenced deliberately around two real constraints — a demanding role and a new baby. The aim of Year 1 is not scale; it is proof, systems and a reputation, built in protected pockets of time, with no pressure to replace a salary.

Phase 0 · Foundation — now

Brand, site & compliance live

Premium site published; legal scope, disclaimers, insurance and autónomo status in place; intake form and AI report workflow stood up and tested. Outcome: ready to take a first client safely.

Phase 1 · Founding clients — months 1–3

5–10 clients, at a founder's rate

Serve a first cohort — ideally from the warm network — refine the report and the session, and gather honest testimonials and the first five-star reviews. Outcome: proof the offer works and the price holds.

Phase 2 · Rhythm — months 4–8

Referral network & a steady trickle

Build 3–5 referral relationships; publish a little credible content; let memberships begin to accumulate. Move to full pricing. Outcome: a predictable, low-effort flow of the right enquiries.

Phase 3 · Leverage — months 9–12+

Systems compounding, options opening

The flywheel — review → referral → call → client — turns with less push. Evaluate the first one-to-many offer (a group cohort or a course) and a possible price rise. Outcome: a real asset, and a credible path beyond employment if desired.

Milestones worth celebrating

First free call booked · first paying client · first unsolicited five-star review · first referral from a partner · first recurring membership · first month the practice covers its own costs · first client who says "I feel like myself again."

A permission, not a pressure Because there is no investor clock and no salary to replace, the roadmap can flex around life. A quiet quarter around the birth is not a failure — the systems hold, the reviews remain, and momentum resumes when capacity does. That resilience is a feature of the model, by design.
Fig. 08 — Legal, Risk & Compliance

Built on the right side of every line

For a science-credentialled founder, rigorous compliance is not a constraint — it is part of the brand promise. This section sets the guardrails; none of it is legal advice, and the items flagged below should be confirmed with a Spanish gestor and, where noted, a lawyer.

Scope of practice — the non-negotiable boundary

In Spain, "dietista-nutricionista" is a legally protected, regulated health profession (Ley 44/2003, LOPS). The founder is not a registered dietitian or a physician, and the practice must never present itself as either. Auralis Natura operates as holistic health & wellness coaching and education: lifestyle, habits, general nutrition education, accountability and wellbeing — not diagnosis, not treatment of disease, not prescriptive medical nutrition therapy. "Dr." is disclosed transparently as a PhD in bioorganic chemistry, never as a medical title. Held cleanly, this boundary is also a differentiator: it signals exactly the integrity a science brand is built on.

The compliance checklist

Business registration

Register as autónomo (self-employed) with Hacienda and Social Security; budget the monthly cuota de autónomos and quarterly IRPF (income tax) filings. A gestor handles this for a modest monthly fee — already costed in the P&L. Confirm current rates & any new-autónomo reductions with a gestor.

VAT (IVA)

Some health & education services are IVA-exempt in Spain, but coaching is not automatically exempt. Treatment of IVA depends on the precise service definition and must be verified with the gestor before invoicing — getting this right from day one avoids painful corrections later.

GDPR & health data

Intake data is special-category health data under GDPR — the highest tier of protection. Requirements: explicit consent, a clear privacy policy, a lawful basis, data minimisation, secure (ideally EU-hosted, encrypted) storage, and a defined retention period. The AI workflow must be designed so personal data is handled lawfully at every step.

Professional liability insurance

Carry professional indemnity / civil liability cover appropriate to health-adjacent coaching (budgeted in the P&L). It protects the practice and signals seriousness to partners and clients alike.

Client contract & terms

Every engagement begins with written terms: scope (coaching, not medical care), what is and isn't included, payment & cancellation policy, confidentiality, and an explicit informed-consent and disclaimer clause. Have the template reviewed once by a lawyer.

Disclaimers everywhere

Consistent "this is education, not medical advice — consult your doctor; in an emergency call 112" language on the site, in reports, and in terms. The red-flag triage in the workflow ensures anyone who should see a physician is told to, promptly and clearly.

Fig. 09 — Founder & Time Model

Building a practice beside a career and a baby

This venture's defining constraint is also its design brief: it must be buildable in limited, protected, unpredictable hours — and it must never become a second source of burnout. The whole model is shaped to honour that.

The governing principle

Protect the founder first. A practice that drains the person at its centre cannot deliver energy and wellbeing to anyone else.

Every system exists to convert scarce hours into maximum value — and to make it safe to step back when life requires it.

Protected, bounded hours

Define a realistic weekly envelope — perhaps 4–6 hours in the early phase — and hold sessions only within set windows. The capacity model (Document 02, §3) is deliberately built to fit this, not to exceed it.

Asynchronous by default

Intake, AI drafting, invoicing and review prompts all happen without live presence. Only the call and the report walkthrough need the founder in real time — so the calendar stays light and forgiving.

Lever 01

Price for value, not hours

Premium pricing means fewer clients for the same income — fewer hours, deeper work, less fatigue. The plan chooses depth over volume on purpose.

Lever 02

Systems do the repeatable

Templates, SOPs and the AI workflow absorb everything that doesn't need human judgement, so the founder's energy goes only where it's irreplaceable.

Lever 03

Permission to pause

No investors, no salary to replace. Around the birth, the practice can idle gracefully and restart intact. Sustainability is the strategy.

A practice that models its own promise There is a quiet integrity in a wellbeing business that is itself built on balance. Clients can sense it — and it keeps the founder's own energy, the very thing she is selling, intact for the long run.
