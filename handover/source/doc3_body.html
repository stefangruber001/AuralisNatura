Fig. 01 — The Operating Philosophy

AI-assisted, human-led, safe by design

Before the tools and the steps, three principles. Every operational decision in this document flows from them — and any future tool or shortcut should be tested against them.

Principle 01

The founder's time is the scarce resource

Everything that does not require Dr. Gruber's specific judgement or presence should be done by a system. Her hours are reserved for the conversation and the clinical thinking — the parts no tool can replace.

Principle 02

AI drafts; the human decides

AI is a brilliant first-draft writer and synthesiser, never the final authority. Every report passes through a human checkpoint where the founder reviews, edits and approves before anything reaches a client. No exceptions.

Principle 03

Safety is the first feature

The workflow is built to recognise when something is beyond coaching and to send the client to a doctor — clearly and early. Educational, never diagnostic. This protects the client, the founder, and the brand.

The shape of the whole system

A premium, deeply personal experience for the client — produced by an engine that protects the founder's time and energy, and never compromises on safety or scientific honesty.

Why this is a competitive moat Most solo coaches either burn out doing everything by hand, or scale by cutting the personal touch. This model does neither: it keeps the human warmth and rigour exactly where they matter, and automates only the mechanics. That combination — premium and personal, yet efficient and safe — is very hard to copy.

Fig. 03 — Step 1 · Intake

Gathering the right story, safely

The intake form is the foundation of the whole engine: the quality of the report depends entirely on the quality of what is gathered here. It should feel calm and professional, take ten minutes, and quietly do three jobs — inform the report, protect the client, and protect the practice.

What a great intake form collects

Section A · Who they are & why they came

Name, age, location, language preference; their main goal in their own words; what prompted them to reach out now; what they have already tried. This is the emotional core the report must speak to.

Section B · Health & lifestyle picture

Energy, sleep, stress, digestion, mood; typical daily eating & movement; life stage (e.g. postpartum, perimenopause); current supplements. Enough context to personalise — without overreaching into diagnosis.

Section C · Safety & medical context

Diagnosed conditions, medications, allergies, pregnancy status, and a short set of red-flag screening questions (see Fig. 05). This is where the form quietly decides whether coaching is appropriate or a doctor is needed first.

Section D · Consent & expectations

Explicit GDPR consent for processing health data; a plain-language scope statement (coaching & education, not medical care); and agreement to the terms. Signed before any work begins.

Intake design principles

Make it feel like care, not paperwork. Warm microcopy, logical grouping, and a progress indicator. Ask only what the report will use — every extra field lowers completion and adds GDPR burden (data minimisation). Use the client's own words with open text boxes for goals and concerns; these become the most personal lines in the report. Build the safety questions in so screening is automatic, not an afterthought. And store it securely — this is special-category health data.

Best practice 01

One thing per screen, early wins first

Start with easy, motivating questions (their goal, their story) before the detailed health history. Momentum lifts completion rates.

Best practice 02

Mostly structured, a little open

Structured fields (dropdowns, scales) make the AI's job reliable; a few open boxes capture the human nuance. Balance both.

Best practice 03

Set expectations at the end

Close with what happens next and when ("Desiree will personally review this and prepare your report within X days"). Anticipation, managed.

Tooling A GDPR-friendly form tool (see Fig. 09) sent automatically when a paid client books. Responses flow into one place the founder reviews before the AI step — so she always sees the human before the machine does.
Fig. 04 — Step 2 · The AI Report Engine

The core IP — draft, review, generate

This is the heart of the operation and the practice's true differentiator: a system that turns a completed intake into a beautiful, evidence-informed, deeply personalised report — drafted by AI, perfected by a human, and rendered at premium quality in a single click.

How the engine works — four movements

Movement 01 · Input

Intake becomes a structured brief

The client's completed intake — their story, goals, health picture and context — is assembled into a clean brief that the AI receives. Nothing is invented; everything traces back to what the client actually said.

Movement 02 · Draft

AI writes an evidence-informed first draft

Guided by a carefully engineered prompt (below), the AI produces a structured draft: a warm summary of where the client is, the themes it sees, educational explanations grounded in current credible sources, and a practical, prioritised set of lifestyle and nutrition suggestions.

Movement 03 · Review

The founder edits & approves — the human checkpoint

Dr. Gruber reads the entire draft with her expert eye: correcting, deepening, removing anything unsupported, adding her judgement and voice, and confirming the safety triage. Nothing proceeds until she approves. This is where her PhD-level rigour becomes the product.

Movement 04 · Generate

One click renders the premium report

Once approved, the text flows into a branded template — the same "Modern Materia Medica" design system as this document — and is rendered as a high-resolution, beautifully typeset PDF, ready to send. Polish without manual layout work.

The mechanic the founder asked for

AI proposes a draft she can freely edit. When she is happy with it, a single click turns her approved words into the final, high-resolution, premium-looking report. The machine does the typing and the typesetting; she does the thinking and the deciding.

The engine's instruction set (illustrative system prompt)

The AI is governed by a carefully written instruction set that bakes in voice, rigour and — above all — safety. An illustrative shape:

System prompt — illustrative

▸ Role: You draft a personalised holistic-health education report for a client of Auralis Natura, founded by a PhD chemist and certified holistic-health coach.

▸ You are educational, never diagnostic. Do not name diseases as conclusions, do not prescribe medication or medical nutrition therapy, do not contradict a doctor. Frame everything as general wellbeing education and lifestyle guidance.

▸ Safety first: If the intake shows any red-flag sign (see triage list), open the report by clearly recommending the client consult a physician, and keep all suggestions gentle and general.

▸ Evidence & honesty: Ground claims in current, credible science. Where evidence is weak or mixed, say so plainly. Never overstate. Prefer "may support" to "will fix".

▸ Voice: Warm, intelligent, calm, precise — like a brilliant friend who happens to be a scientist. Use the client's own words where possible.

▸ Structure: Follow the six-section report architecture. Prioritise; never overwhelm. End with clear, realistic next steps.

▸ Always: Produce a draft for human review. You are a first-draft writer; the founder edits and approves everything before it reaches a client.

Why the design system is the secret weapon Because the report renders through the same branded HTML-to-PDF template used for this very document, every client receives something that looks like it came from a top-tier consultancy — without the founder ever touching a layout tool. The premium deliverable is the brand, produced at near-zero marginal effort.
Fig. 05 — The Safety & Scope Layer

Knowing when to refer out

This is the most important section in the document. A holistic-health coach is not a doctor, and the single greatest risk — to the client and the practice — is failing to recognise when something needs medical care. The workflow is built so that recognition is systematic, not left to memory.

The golden rule

When in doubt, refer out. Coaching complements medical care; it never replaces it. No revenue is worth working outside scope or delaying a client's access to a doctor. Held consistently, this is not a limitation — it is the foundation of the trust the entire brand is built on.

Red-flag triage — signs that mandate a doctor first

These are screened at intake and re-checked at the AI and review stages. Any one present means the report leads with a referral and all guidance stays gentle and general. This list is illustrative and should be finalised with a medical professional.

Checkpoint 01 · Intake

Screen at the door

Red-flag questions are built into the form, so concerning signs surface before any coaching work begins.

Checkpoint 02 · AI

Flag in the draft

The engine is instructed to detect red flags and lead with a referral rather than burying it — a safety net, not the final word.

Checkpoint 03 · Human

The founder confirms

Dr. Gruber's review is the decisive safety check. Her judgement, not the AI's, determines whether and how to proceed.

What the practice never does It does not diagnose disease, prescribe or adjust medication, provide medical nutrition therapy for a clinical condition, or tell a client to stop a doctor's treatment. It educates, supports healthy habits, and refers — and says so clearly, every time. (See also the scope & compliance guardrails in Documents 01 and 02.)
Fig. 06 — Step 3 · Deliver & Discuss

The report and the conversation

The report is the artefact; the conversation is where it comes alive. Together they turn information into the feeling that someone truly understands you and has given you a way forward.

The delivery

The approved report is sent by email from the founder, in a short, warm, personal note — never an automated-feeling drop. The client opens a beautiful, premium PDF with their name on it. The moment of receiving it should feel like a small event, not a transaction.

The conversation

A live session walks through the report together: reflecting back their story, explaining the "why", agreeing the first realistic steps, and answering questions. This is where coaching happens — accountability, encouragement, and the human relationship that keeps clients coming back.

A simple session structure

01 · Reconnect

Open warmly

Revisit their goal and how they're feeling today. Set the tone: this is their hour.

02 · Reflect

Walk the report

Move through the findings, checking understanding and resonance as you go.

03 · Prioritise

Agree first steps

Choose two or three realistic actions to begin — not the whole plan at once.

04 · Invite

Offer the path on

Where it fits, gently introduce the programme or membership that supports the journey.

The conversion moment, handled with grace Because the report has already delivered real value, the invitation to continue feels like a natural next step rather than a sales pitch. Value first, offer second — the most trustworthy way to grow a premium practice.
Fig. 07 — Step 4 · Invoice & Payment

Getting paid, gracefully

Payment should be as polished and frictionless as everything else — a clean branded invoice, an easy online payment, and bookkeeping that takes care of itself. Money handled well is part of a premium experience, not separate from it.

01 · Invoice

Clean & branded

A professional, Auralis-branded invoice generated from a template — clear line items, terms, and tax details handled correctly for an autónomo.

02 · Pay

Easy online payment

A simple payment link or card option. For programmes, instalments (e.g. three monthly payments) lower the barrier on higher-value packages.

03 · Record

Books that keep themselves

Each payment flows to bookkeeping for the quarterly IRPF and VAT handling — ideally synced to the tools the gestor uses, so nothing is manual at quarter-end.

Payment timing options

Two clean models: pay upfront at booking (best for single sessions and cashflow), or pay on delivery alongside the report session. For multi-week programmes, instalments make premium prices feel accessible without discounting.

Keep it premium

No clunky chasing, no awkward conversations. Clear terms agreed up front (in the intake) mean payment is simply expected and easy. A gracious, confident approach to money signals a practice that knows its worth.

Compliance note Invoicing, VAT (IVA) treatment and bookkeeping must follow Spanish autónomo rules — confirm the correct invoice format and tax handling with the gestor (see Document 02, Fig. 08). Set the templates up correctly once, and every future invoice is effortless and compliant.
Fig. 08 — Step 5 · The Review Loop

Turning delight into proof & referrals

A five-star review is the most valuable marketing asset a premium practice can own — it converts the next free call before it even happens. The final step closes the loop by capturing that proof at exactly the right moment.

01 · Time it well

Ask at peak satisfaction

The invitation lands shortly after the report session — when the client feels most understood and hopeful. Timing is everything; ask too early or too late and the magic fades.

02 · Make it effortless

One warm, simple ask

A short, genuine message with a direct link to leave a review on the website. The easier it is, the more happy clients actually do it.

03 · Put it to work

Reviews fuel the funnel

Reviews appear on the site's testimonials section, lifting the conversion of every future visitor. Delight becomes proof becomes the next client.

The flywheel, closed

A delighted client leaves a review · the review wins the next free call · the call becomes a client · who is delighted in turn. Each happy client makes the next one easier to find.

Reviews and referrals are cousins The same moment that earns a review is the moment to gently invite a referral — "if you know someone who'd benefit, I'd be glad to offer them a free call too." Two assets from one moment of delight.
Authenticity only Reviews are always real and freely given — never incentivised or fabricated. A premium brand's credibility depends on it, and genuine words from real clients are far more persuasive than anything invented.

The GDPR lens on every tool

Because health data is involved, prefer tools that offer EU data residency, encryption, a data-processing agreement, and clear deletion controls. When using AI on client information, ensure the arrangement does not train on or retain personal data, and minimise what is sent. Choosing well here is a one-time decision that protects the practice for years.

Start lean, add only when it hurts

Begin with the fewest tools that deliver the five steps well. Add automation only when a manual step becomes a genuine bottleneck — not before. Simplicity is cheaper, safer, and easier to run solo.

Connect the steps

The value compounds when booking → intake → report → invoice → review flow into one another with minimal re-keying. Light connections between tools save the founder's most precious resource: time.

Fig. 11 — Putting It Together

The company operating system

Step back, and the whole operation is a single, elegant loop — a system that takes a curious visitor and returns a delighted client and a five-star review, while protecting the founder's time, energy and integrity at every turn.

The operating system in one line

Free call → intake → AI-drafted, human-approved report → delivery & conversation → invoice → five-star review → which wins the next free call.

What to build first — a pragmatic sequence

Build 01

The essentials to take one client safely

Scheduling, the intake form (with safety questions & consent), the report template, and a way to invoice. Enough to deliver the full journey by hand for the first few clients.

Build 02

The AI report engine & prompt

Stand up the drafting step and the system prompt; render through the brand template. This is where the time-saving and the premium feel really begin.

Build 03

The review & referral loop

Add the well-timed review invitation and the site testimonials. Now the flywheel can start turning on its own.

Build 04

The connections & polish

Link the steps, tidy the SOPs, refine the prompts. Each improvement compounds — less founder effort, same or better quality.

The promise of the system

A practice that delivers world-class, deeply personal care — built and run by one scientist, in protected hours, beside a career and a new baby, without ever compromising on safety, rigour, or warmth.

The whole package, working as one This operating system, the strategy in Document 01, the business model in Document 02, and the website all point the same way: toward a calm, credible, premium practice that fits a life — and grows, one beautiful report and one five-star review at a time.
