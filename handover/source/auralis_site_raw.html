Fig. 06 — The founder

A scientist who chose to treat the whole person.

For over ten years I made sure medicines met the highest standard of quality. Then I wanted to bring that same standard to something closer to home: how we actually feel, every day.

My name is Desiree Gruber. I hold a PhD in bioorganic chemistry and spent a decade in pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical quality — most recently leading global quality for advanced therapies at Novartis. Precision, evidence and the refusal to cut corners are simply how I think.

But the science of the body doesn't end at the lab door. Becoming a mother, and seeing how often people are handed generic advice for deeply personal struggles, led me to formally train in holistic health and nutrition at the Academy of Naturopathy, with specialist certification in women's health. Auralis Natura is where those two worlds meet — rigorous science, in service of a more natural, energetic life.

I built this practice to give people what I always wanted for the ones I love: to be properly listened to, taken seriously, and given a plan that's genuinely theirs.
