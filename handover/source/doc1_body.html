Fig. 00 — Executive Summary

The opportunity in one page

Auralis Natura enters a large, fast-growing wellness-coaching market at the exact point where three powerful currents meet: the shift from reactive medicine to preventive health, the demand for personalisation over generic diet plans, and a rising, underserved appetite for women's hormonal and life-stage health. Its defensible edge is rare and almost impossible to copy — a PhD bioorganic chemist with a decade of pharmaceutical quality leadership, now formally trained in holistic nutrition and women's health.

The strategy in five moves

  1. Position as a premium holistic health coach & educator — never a clinician — converting Desiree's scientific authority into trust, not into regulated medical claims.
  2. Win a beachhead: ambitious women navigating energy, hormones and life-stage change (fertility, pregnancy, postpartum, perimenopause) — a premium, growing, under-served niche that matches her credentials and lived experience.
  3. Lead with a free 25-minute call as the single conversion engine, then ascend clients through a clear ladder of packages and a membership.
  4. Make the deliverable the moat — a beautiful, scientifically-reasoned personal report, produced with an AI-assisted workflow and personally reviewed, that feels worth multiples of its price.
  5. Build for a solo founder–new mother: asynchronous, systemised, low-overhead operations that protect time and scale revenue without scaling hours one-for-one.

Fig. 01 — The 5 C's · Company

An advantage that cannot be copied

Most wellness coaches share one of two backgrounds: clinical or lifestyle. Auralis Natura is built on a third, far rarer foundation — deep laboratory science fused with holistic training and a credible reason to care about women's health.

The founder's stack

Three credentials that rarely coexist

  • PhD, Bioorganic Chemistry — the ability to read primary research, judge evidence quality, and reason about biochemistry and physiology at source.
  • 10+ years pharmaceutical quality leadership (to Global Product Quality Lead, Novartis) — discipline, rigour, and a hard-wired instinct for safety, traceability and process.
  • Certified in holistic health, nutrition & women's health (Frauenheilkunde), 97.22% exam — the formal, client-facing competence to translate science into lifestyle guidance.

Add a Yoga & Meditation Instructor certification and four working languages (German, English, Spanish, Italian), and the result is a genuinely differentiated, multilingual, premium operator.

The core thesis

"Evidence before advice — a plan built around your body, not the latest diet."

The brand's entire promise is that recommendations are checked, not repeated. That is something a PhD can credibly claim and a typical coach cannot.

Founder–market fit Desiree is building this while having a baby. Rather than a constraint to hide, this is authentic proof of the women's-health positioning — she is living the exact life-stage transition her beachhead clients face.

Fig. 02 — The 5 C's · Customers

Start narrow to grow wide

A new premium practice cannot be "for everyone." The fastest path to authority and referrals is a sharp beachhead, then deliberate expansion into adjacent segments that share the same core job.

Beachhead

Women in life-stage transition

Fertility, pregnancy, postpartum, cycle & perimenopause. High motivation, high stakes, under-served by generic plans — and the strongest fit for Desiree's credentials and story.

Expansion A

Depleted high-performers

Professionals with low energy, brain fog, poor sleep and "normal" bloodwork who want a rigorous, root-cause approach rather than another supplement.

Expansion B

Prevention-minded & expat

Internationally-minded, health-literate clients (incl. English/German/Italian-speaking expats in Spain) who value science and will pay for a premium, personal service.

The ideal client profile (ICP)

Who they are
  • Woman, ~30–50, educated, time-poor, financially comfortable.
  • In or near a hormonal / life-stage shift (or simply exhausted and unheard).
  • Has tried the apps, plans and supplements — and is done guessing.
  • Trusts evidence and credentials over hype; sceptical of fad diets.
  • Comfortable with online, premium services and wants a real person, not a chatbot.
What they value & fear

Values: being properly listened to, a clear plan that fits a busy real life, scientific honesty, discretion, and a sense of calm rather than restriction.

Fears: wasting money on another generic program, being dismissed again, judgement, complexity she has no time for, and unsafe advice during a sensitive life stage.

Willingness to pay: high Price sensitivity: low Trust threshold: high
Why this beachhead wins It is premium (life-stage health commands higher prices), under-served (most coaches are generalists; few combine science + women's-health depth), referable (women share trusted health resources within close networks), and authentic (Desiree is living it). Authority earned here transfers naturally to the expansion segments.

The positioning map

Two axes that matter

Vertical — Scientific rigour (hype ⟶ evidence).
Horizontal — Care style (clinical/transactional ⟶ holistic/personal).

Apps and influencers cluster low-rigour. Clinics sit high-rigour but clinical-cold. Holistic coaches sit personal but lower-rigour. The top-right quadrant — high rigour and high warmth — is largely empty. That is Auralis Natura's home.

What to learn from the closest analog

Lessons from science-led peers

  • The free first call works — it lowers the barrier and lets credibility do the selling. Keep it central.
  • Multilingual is a moat — serving several languages widens the funnel dramatically online.
  • Personal story sells trust — a clear founder narrative outperforms feature lists.
  • Niche beats general — a specific focus (e.g. gut, hormones) earns authority faster than "everything wellness."

Fig. 05 — The 5 C's · Climate

Strong tailwinds, one firm line

The macro environment is unusually favourable. The single most important external factor to design around is regulatory: the line between coaching/education and regulated clinical practice.

Tailwinds (why now)

  • Prevention & longevity — a cultural shift from treating illness to optimising health and energy.
  • Personalisation — demand for plans built around the individual, not population averages.
  • Women's-health awakening — fast-growing investment and attention on hormones, fertility, postpartum & menopause.
  • Online-first delivery — clients now expect and prefer remote, premium coaching.
  • AI-assisted personalisation — tools make a beautiful, tailored deliverable feasible for a solo founder.
  • Trust premium — post-misinformation, credible, evidence-based voices command attention and price.
Regulatory line — design constraint #1

In Spain the title "dietista-nutricionista" is legally protected (Ley 44/2003 de Ordenación de las Profesiones Sanitarias). Desiree is not a registered dietitian or physician. Auralis Natura must therefore present itself as a holistic health & wellness coaching and education service — supporting lifestyle, habits, energy and general wellbeing — and must not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or claim to cure disease.

  • "Dr." is always framed transparently as a PhD in bioorganic chemistry — explicitly not a medical qualification.
  • Language stays in the coaching register — "support," "educate," "guide," "general wellbeing"; never "treat," "diagnose," "cure," "prescribe."
  • A refer-out reflex is built in — red-flag symptoms, pregnancy complications, medication questions and clinical conditions are directed to qualified healthcare professionals.
  • GDPR & sensitive-data care — health information is processed lawfully, with consent, minimisation and security (detailed in the Business Plan & Operations docs).
  • Clear disclaimers everywhere — website, reports, intake and contracts state the educational, non-medical nature of the service.
Reframe The boundary is not a limitation — it is a positioning gift. "We work alongside your doctor, never instead of them" is exactly what a premium, trustworthy, science-led brand should say. Safety is the brand.
Fig. 06 — Jobs To Be Done

What clients are really "hiring" for

People don't buy nutrition coaching; they hire it to make progress in their lives. Mapping the functional, emotional and social jobs reveals what the offer, the report and the messaging must deliver.

The core job

"Help me finally feel like myself again — clear, energetic and at home in my own body — with someone I can trust."

Everything below is in service of this one outcome. The diet is a means; feeling well and in control is the job.

Functional jobs
  • Understand what my body actually needs, specific to me.
  • Get a clear, do-able plan that fits a busy life.
  • Make sense of conflicting information from one trusted source.
  • Increase energy, sleep & focus; ease symptoms.
  • Navigate a specific life stage safely (e.g. postpartum).
Emotional jobs
  • Feel genuinely heard, not dismissed in ten minutes.
  • Replace guilt and overwhelm with calm and agency.
  • Trust that the advice is safe and evidence-based.
  • Feel cared for by a real expert, not a generic app.
  • Regain a sense of hope and control.
Social jobs
  • Show up as a better partner, parent & professional.
  • Be the kind of person who invests wisely in their health.
  • Have a credible answer ("I work with a PhD-trained coach").
  • Recommend something they're proud to share.

Fig. 07 — Problem → Solution

The architecture of the offer

Auralis Natura exists to resolve four recurring failures of the current market. Each problem has a designed response that becomes a pillar of the brand.

Problem 01

"I've tried everything and still feel depleted."

Plans, apps and supplements that never addressed the root or the whole person.

Solution

Root-cause, whole-person method — connecting nutrition, sleep, stress and rhythm into one clear picture before recommending anything.

Problem 02

"I'm drowning in contradictory advice."

One source says fast, another says graze; guilt and guesswork fill the gap.

Solution

One trusted, evidence-based voice — a PhD who checks claims against research and translates them into calm, clear guidance.

Problem 03

"I was seen for ten minutes and sent away."

Bloodwork "normal," symptoms real, nobody connected the dots.

Solution

Deep listening + a personal report — generous intake, a real plan, and a relationship that continues beyond a single appointment.

Problem 04

"My body changed the rules and my habits don't fit."

Pregnancy, postpartum, a hormonal shift or a demanding career.

Solution

Life-stage specialism — women's-health-aware coaching that adapts to seasons, always alongside the client's medical care.
